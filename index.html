          Good Questions. Clear Answers.

          Plain answers about the cases and what homeowners can do. Nothing here is legal advice.

          If you're a homeowner

          My roof claim was denied or underpaid. What can I do?

          Ask your insurer for your claim file and the adjuster's estimate in writing, and keep every letter, email and photo. You can file a complaint with the Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions at 602-364-2499. You also have the right to talk with an attorney of your choosing.

          This site can't tell you whether you have a case.

          How should I document roof damage?

          As soon as it's safe, photograph the roof, gutters, vents, siding and windows from the ground, and note the date and time. Keep the storm date, any hail reports for your area and any roofer's inspection report. Don't climb on the roof yourself.

          Who can I call right now?

          The Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions takes consumer questions and complaints at 602-364-2499.

          If I share my story, am I joining a lawsuit?

          No. Sharing your story doesn't make you part of any case and doesn't make you anyone's client. We read every submission. We only share a story publicly if you check the box that allows it.
